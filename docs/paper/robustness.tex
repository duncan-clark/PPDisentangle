\section{Robustness simulation checks}\label{sec:robustness-simulations}

This section is generated from the robustness simulation pipeline. The pipeline
re-runs the Hawkes simulation study over varying branching-ratio separations,
signal-to-noise scalings, joint shrinkage of signal and treatment separation,
kernel misspecification checks, off-support allocation contrasts, label recovery
metrics, and the forward-only decay-validation diagnostic.
